Export Compliance & Rules

International Shipping
Terms & Conditions

Prior to making your purchase, you are responsible to ensure that the food product can be legally shipped and imported into your designated country. You may also check out from your local Customs office for additional information.

1 Acceptance of Terms

By placing an order for international shipping on our platform, you agree to comply with and be bound by the terms and conditions outlined below. These terms constitute a legally binding agreement between you ("the customer") and Local Export+ ("we," "us," or "our").

2 Shipping Policy

2.1 Delivery Address: Shipments cannot be delivered to PO boxes or postal codes. Shipments are delivered to the consignee's address given by customer upon checkout and payment made successfully.

2.2 Shipping Methods: We offer international shipping services using trusted courier partners.

2.3 Inspection: Couriers hold the right to open and inspect a shipment without notice for safety, security, customs or other regulatory reasons.

2.4 Shipping Charges: Shipping charges are calculated based on factors including the destination country, package volumetric weight, and actual weight, displayed clearly during checkout.

2.5 Customs and Duties: If the value of your order exceeds the de minimis value in your destination country, the customer is responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees imposed by the destination country prior to delivery.

3 Prohibited & Restricted Food Items

All packages are subject to Customs inspection and clearance. To avoid confiscation, the following items will NOT BE ACCEPTED for international shipping:

  • Fresh & Frozen Foods: Perishable items requiring refrigeration or specific environmental control.
  • Restricted Permits: Sugar, rice, and grains (including rice flour, rice polishing, rice bran, and rice vermicelli).
  • All products in liquid forms.
  • Any meat, bones, skin, or animal/poultry derivatives.
  • Medication or pharmaceutical products without supporting doctor prescriptions.

4 Order Processing & Delivery

4.1 Processing: We strive to process orders promptly upon receipt. Parcels ship within 3 to 5 business days from Malaysia.

4.2 Delivery Times: Estimated timelines are general guidelines. Actual delivery may vary due to customs processing or local holidays.

4.3 Tracking: Tracking information is provided via email notification once picked up successfully.

5 Changes, Cancellations & Refunds

5.1 Changes: After placing an order, changes to addresses or details may not be feasible.

5.2 Cancellations: Must be submitted promptly. Once picked up and shipped, cancellation is not possible.

5.3 Refunds: Shipping charges are generally non-refundable unless a shipping-related error on our part occurs.

6 Limitation of Liability & Contact

We are not liable for any damages, losses, or liabilities arising from delays, customs clearance, or delivery issues beyond our control (Force Majeure).
